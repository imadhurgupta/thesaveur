Who We Are

HM Enterprises — Born from a Passion for Purity

HM Enterprises, operating under the brand The Saveur, was founded with a single unwavering belief: that every Indian household deserves access to genuinely pure, high-quality food products — not the mass-produced, adulterated versions that flood the market.

Based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, HM Enterprises is a FSSAI-certified food company (License No: 12226999000440) supplying premium spices, herbal teas, gourmet makhana, and artisanal food products to households and businesses across 28+ states in India.

Every batch sourced by HM Enterprises is rigorously tested, carefully packaged, and delivered with care — a promise of authenticity and quality.
